# Privacy Policy - Mao Said

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Mao Said collects no personal data.

## Data Collection

Mao Said does not collect, transmit, or store personal data. The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting SDKs.

## Network Access

The app does not make network requests during normal use. Quotes and background images are bundled locally within the app, and an internet connection is not required.

## Local Storage

The app stores only one preference on your device: the selected swipe direction. This preference never leaves your device and is stored using the operating system's standard preferences API.

## Third-Party Code

Mao Said uses app framework libraries to render the interface and store local preferences. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash reporting SDKs, and it does not share data with third-party services.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children. It contains no user accounts, communication features, or in-app purchases.
