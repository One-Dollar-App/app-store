Privacy Policy - Mao Said

Last updated: August 12, 2026

The developer of Mao Said does not collect personal data from the app.

Data Collection

Mao Said does not send personal notes, reading activity, device identifiers, or other personal data to the developer or to an app-operated server. The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting SDKs.

Network Access

The app itself does not make network requests during normal use. Quotes, reference notes, and background images are bundled with the app, and an internet connection is not required.

If you choose Share, Mao Said opens the iOS system share sheet with the selected quote, source, and date. Personal notes are not included. Any service you select from the system share sheet processes the shared content under that service's own privacy policy.

Local Storage

Mao Said stores the following information in the app's local storage using the operating system's standard preferences API:

  • Selected swipe direction
  • Favorite quote identifiers
  • Recently opened quote identifiers
  • Personal notes that you choose to save for individual quotes

Mao Said has no account system or app-operated cloud sync, and the developer does not receive this local information. Local app data may be included in device backups managed by the operating system according to your backup settings. Removing a favorite or saving a blank note removes that item from the app's local storage. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device, subject to any backups controlled by you or the operating system.

Third-Party Code

Mao Said uses framework libraries to render the interface and store local preferences. These libraries do not provide the developer with analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting data through the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children. It contains no user accounts, communication features, or in-app purchases.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: hi@one-dollar.app
